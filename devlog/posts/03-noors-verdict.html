We took 12 levels, walked through them at player height, screenshotted everything with fixed camera rules (no cherry-picking), and asked Noor, our resident art brain, for an honest review.

"BadLevel generates spaces. It doesn't yet generate convincing architecture."Noor

Ouch. Also: completely right. The rooms worked, but they were boxes. Her vote was "architectural identity and structural variety next", so that's what the evening became:

  • Hall cross-sections: barrel vaults, pointed vaults, basilicas with aisles, trusses, sawtooth roofs, coffered ceilings. Smooth, not stepped.
  • Structure along the walls: pilasters on a rhythm, niches between them, an apse at the end of the big hall.
  • Portals: doorways with frames and splayed mouths, so walking from a corridor into a hall feels like an arrival.
