Underground mode: you start in a room open to the sky and go down, storey after storey, to a rough-cut deep chamber at the bottom. Then a little treat: a booth higher up with a slit window looking down into that chamber, so you see where you're going long before you get there.

Compounds: yards with buildings in them. You can go inside, climb to the roofs, and there's a counter and a pickup waiting. The first version made towers. BadSad asked for "lower, wider houses", so we demolished the towers. No towers were harmed. Well, two.
