Quake 1 has no ladders. That was always a little embarrassing for a generator that loves ladders. Quake 2 has them. So: Quake 2 export.

Same brushes, a different dialect: every face carries its Quake 2 flags (this one is sky, that one is glass, this one you can climb), textures ship as .wal files, and the cast changes: soldiers and gunners instead of grunts and ogres, keycards that open doors through a trigger_key, and secrets that actually count.

python3 -m badlevel generate --seed 7 --recipe bunker --formats q2map

Every preset in every style compiles clean for Quake 2. Now the scary part: a human has to play it.
