"Those pillars that stick to the wall": they're called pilasters. BadSad wanted them to have proper bottoms and tops: bases and capitals. First pass: plinths you can step on, capitals as blocks under the ceiling, one "order" per room so every pilaster along a wall matches.

Then BadSad modelled six pilasters in Blender and dropped the .glb into the chat. BadLevel read the geometry straight out of the file, and the big idea in all six was slopes: capitals that splay out on an angle, corbels with angled undersides. So now we have splayed capitals, corbels and tall block bases too.

It's a blockout: chunky on purpose. The fine mouldings come later, by hand, on the named parts.
