One root cause: the map named its textures but shipped no pixels. The rerelease drew those walls as nothing; WinQuake swapped in a placeholder that broke its sky. The fix: BadLevel now makes its own little texture wad, grey grids, one line per 32-unit cell, so your eyes can count distances. Also maps are now centred on the origin, because original Quake can't reach past ±4096 units.
