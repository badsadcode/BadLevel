Every level generator starts the same way: with grey boxes. BadLevel's boxes live on a grid of 32-unit cells (one Quake "step" wide), and here's the trick that made everything after it possible: BadLevel never builds walls. It carves air. Whatever isn't air is solid, so every level is sealed by construction. No leaks. Not one, in 56 commits.

The first morning gave us rooms and corridors, then rooms that aren't rectangles (with real 45-degree walls, not staircase jaggies), corridors three, five and seven cells wide, and a validator: a little ghost that walks every generated level and checks you can actually finish it. Keys before doors, no dead ends you can fall into and never leave, every room reachable.

Then a combat analyser, because a level isn't a level until something can shoot you in it: line of sight in 3D, exposure heat maps, "this corridor is a shooting gallery" warnings.
