The first real playtest report, word for word: "Sometimes I get stuck and can't get through, the pass is too narrow. And are you trying to kill me? So many monsters. I had to cheat. And it's pretty dark in places." Three bugs, one sentence.

  • Stuck: the validator walked the level as a point. Quake's player is a 32-unit box, and a 32-unit gap closes to nothing for it. One bunker had 13 of 15 rooms a real player couldn't reach! Now BadLevel walks every level with a player-sized box and widens every squeeze it finds, without ever opening a shortcut round a locked door.
  • Monsters: they came per square metre, so a big room was a monster pit: 132 in one level, 28 of them Ogres. Now big rooms thin out, and Quake's skill levels work: Easy gets half, Normal three quarters.
  • Dark: lights hung under 20-metre vaults lit nothing. Typical floor brightness went from 15 to 126 (of 255).
