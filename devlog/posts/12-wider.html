BadSad installed Quake 2, loaded the bunker, walked into a corridor and stopped: "I wasn't able to go through. It's too narrow. In general, we'll need wider corridors and more spacious rooms."

Back on E1M8 the deal was "don't lose the tight Quake 1 feel". Two engines and a few playtests later, the verdict changed, so the default changed with it:

  • Roomier by default: corridors went from 86% three-wide to 88% five-wide, and rooms grew by a quarter. The old tight levels are still there, byte for byte, with --space 0.
  • Wide stairwells and shafts: most of the narrow corridors left were the doors of stairwells, lifts and ladders, which were built 3 wide. Now flights are 5 wide and shafts 7.
  • Ramps: stair corridors can become one smooth slope, with the ceiling following along. To the game it's exactly the same heights as the stairs, so a ramp can never trap you.

Bloopers: Quake 2's compiler bounces light around by default, so with the lights tuned for Quake 1, 94% of the level came out pure white (the screenshot looked like a dentist's office). Quake 2 now gets its own lighting. And bigger rooms are harder to fit: one compound took 13 tries to generate, mostly because a locked door couldn't find a straight bit of short, wide corridor to sit in. Now it's never more than 2.
