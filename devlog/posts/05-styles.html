Same layout, different clothes. BadLevel now has eight architectural styles: none, gothic, industrial, bunker, compound, egyptian, sci-fi and castle. Each brings its own vaults, wall rhythms and door frames.

Every styled level also gets one landmark in its biggest space, something to navigate by: a spire, a chimney stack, a water tower, an obelisk, a reactor core. The first spire was 4 cells wide and completely lost in a 40-metre hall ("one more block"), so landmarks now scale with their room.

And everything got names. Every vault, pier and doorway is a named part in the .glb (one object per room, children per element) and a group in TrenchBroom. Want to replace all the gothic vaults with your own mesh? Select by name, swap, done.
