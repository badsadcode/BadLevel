NoorLevel: deep-sea stations

Eight levels made with the station preset (layout_style = "station"): round and octagonal pods on a spine of round pressure tubes (a line, a ring round a hub, or a branching tree), the docking airlock on the top deck, the core alone on the deepest, decks joined by stair tubes only. Plans are coloured by floor height (deeper is darker): green is the airlock (start), yellow the core (exit), red and blue the keys and their doors, purple the secret door.

The GLBs are Y-up metres; serve this folder over HTTP (e.g. python3 -m http.server) to use the 3D view.
