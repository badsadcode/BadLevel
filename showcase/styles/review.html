One layout, eight architectures

The fortress preset, seed 3, generated eight times. The only thing that changes is --style: none, the four styles from the review steps, and the three new vocabularies, egyptian, sci-fi and castle (D28). The layout is the same in all eight: 19 rooms, same corridors, same storeys. That's guaranteed now. Building this page found a bug where a style could make the first attempt invalid and so change the layout, and a test now prevents it. Camera positions come from the same rules for every style.

What to look at

  • The biggest space (the fortress's great hall) changes the most. Gothic gets a basilica arcade, industrial roof trusses, bunker mushroom piers with drop panels, egyptian a forest of hypostyle columns, sci-fi a chamfered hull with frames, castle a trussed roof with tie-beams.
  • Walls and doorways follow the style too: lancet niches or false doors or panel bays; pointed, round, flat or chamfered portal heads; fireplaces, sanctuaries and bulkhead doors as end features. Open the 3D previews and walk around.
  • Any recipe takes any style: badlevel generate --recipe classic --style castle.

What doesn't work yet

  • Courtyards look the same in every style. Architecture doesn't reach open areas yet (they have no ceiling to shape, and their walls aren't articulated by the style). Yard buildings and the roofscape are compound-only.
  • The egyptian "biggest" shot is half blocked by a column. The camera rule picks a far corner, and in a hypostyle hall there's always a column nearby. The preview shows the hall better.
  • Styles share a few profiles (barrel, undercroft and haunched appear in several), so small rooms in different styles can look alike. The grand halls are where styles differ most.
