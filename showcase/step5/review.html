Same 12 seeds and categories. Left: step 4. Right: step 5 (D23). Only the underground levels changed. The other nine are byte-identical to step 4, so their pairs should match exactly.

What works

  • The descent has its own reveal. On the storey above, a side corridor leads to a small observation booth carved into the rock beside the deep chamber, 7–8 m above its floor. Through a long slit at eye height you look down into it (the new overlook shot) before you're allowed in.
  • It can't be used to cheat. The chamber sits behind the locked door. A gallery would have been a way in, so the generator refused to build one. The booth is a dead end, and the slit is 48 units tall, too low for a player, so the lock still holds.
  • It stays a bunker. The first version used a glass pane, and a preset test caught it: the bunker's identity is "firing slits, never glass". Levels that do use glass get a pane instead.
  • 8 of 8 bunker seeds tested get an overlook. All levels validate, 89 tests pass, levels without a style are unchanged, and the crack check is clean (0 pink pixels in 57 shots).

What doesn't work yet

  • It's found, not shown. The booth is a side branch, and nothing on the main path points to it. A player in a hurry walks past it.
  • A slit is a narrow view. It's true to the bunker, but the chamber is seen as a band, not a vista.
  • Only one overlook per level, and only underground.
