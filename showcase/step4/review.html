Step 4: underground, and a reveal camera that follows the corridor

Same 12 seeds and categories. Left: step 3. Right: step 4 (D22). Two changes:

  • Underground levels (the bunker preset). Their layouts changed: the preset now has 3 storeys and a deep chamber.
  • The reveal camera now looks along the corridor's last stretch, as a player arrives, instead of at the hall's centre. The other nine levels have the same geometry as step 3, so their reveal shots differ only because of the camera.

What works

  • It goes down now. You start at the surface, in a room open to the sky (the new surface shot looks up out of it). The critical path steps down one storey at a time and never climbs back. The exit is always on the deepest storey.
  • The deep chamber. At the bottom there's one big hall cut from the rock: a rough, irregular stepped ceiling, rock pillars left standing, and rough-cut walls with recesses of random width, depth and height. It contrasts with the ribbed concrete rooms above. Seeds 1–6: always on the bottom storey, a cavern in 5 of 6 (the other rolled a low barrel vault).
  • Reveals read as arrivals. Looking straight down the corridor, fortress_2 now frames its arched portal with the vault behind it, and the underground reveals look down tight corridors into the rock chambers.
  • A real bug fixed on the way. A locked door could leave a gap above itself on stair corridors (96 units in one bunker). Doors now fill the whole opening, and a test checks every door.
  • All levels validate, 88 tests pass, levels without a style are byte-identical (26/26), and the crack check is clean (0 pink pixels in 51 shots).

What doesn't work yet

  • The surface is just a roofless room. There's no landscape, entrance building or shaft head above ground. It reads as "a pit you came down", no more.
  • The descent happens in connectors. The storeys are joined by the usual stairwells, lifts and ladders. There's no long winding ramp, no tall access shaft you can look down, and the deep chamber isn't visible from above before you reach it.
  • Cavern rock is blocky. The ceiling is deliberately stepped (cut, not poured), so it looks more quarried than natural. Truly natural caves would need sloped triangles, which the exporter can't make yet.
  • Fortress reveals still often end at a wall or terrace across the corridor mouth: the hall's interior gets in the way of the axis.

Questions

  1. Should the deep chamber be visible from above on the way down (a gallery or a window into it)? That would make the descent a reveal of its own.
  2. Next: open compounds with free-standing buildings, or richer member shapes (buttresses, capitals)?
