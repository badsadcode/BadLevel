Step 8: one deliberate landmark per level

Same 12 seeds and categories. Left: step 7. Right: step 8 (D29). Each level's biggest space now has a landmark, shaped by the level's style. There's a new landmark shot from the far side of its room. Some layouts may differ slightly from step 7, because a bug fix in the same session (D28) stopped architecture from sometimes forcing a regenerated layout. Nothing was picked or touched up.

What works

  • Something to navigate by. The first review said "landmarks" were only the most visible rooms, measured. Now there's an actual object: a stepped spire in the fortress halls, a chimney stack rising into the industrial trusses, a water tower (a tank on a shaft) in the compounds, a monolith in the bunker's deep chamber. The new styles have theirs too: an obelisk (egyptian), a reactor core with floating rings (sci-fi), a crenellated keep (castle).
  • Sized to the room. A first version at 4×4 cells was lost in a 40 m hall full of decks and piers: one more block. Landmarks now scale with their room (8×8 at the base in the mega-halls).
  • Seen and safe. In the four themed presets (seeds 1–5) 19 of 20 levels have one, and all 19 can be seen from a way into their room (tested). It never blocks a doorway or splits the floor, and never breaks a vault. It's a named part you can replace (.landmark in the .glb, a TrenchBroom group in the .map).
  • All levels validate, 105 tests pass, levels without a style are unchanged, and the crack check is clean (0 pink pixels in 73 shots).

What doesn't work yet

  • The bunker's monolith is small (about 4 m). The deep chamber is narrower, and its rough cavern ceiling is low, so only the 1× shape fits. The chamber itself is the bunker's real landmark.
  • The camera often has a pillar or wall in the foreground: it stands at the far side of the room, where the walls and members are. The 3D preview shows the landmark better.
  • Halls whose floor is already a forest of columns (hypostyle, mushroom, multispan) sometimes get no landmark: there's nowhere to put one without crowding.
