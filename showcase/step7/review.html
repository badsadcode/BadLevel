Step 7: room around the yards, and a little life in the buildings

Same 12 seeds and categories. Left: step 6. Right: step 7 (D25). Only the compound levels changed. Their layouts shifted a little, because yards now keep more distance from other rooms, so some viewpoints moved. Nothing was picked or touched up.

What works

  • A sky over the yard. I measured why step 6's roofs didn't show, and it wasn't a lack of room: 88% of the band around each yard was already carved. The problem was the shape. The roofs rose outwards in steps, which from the ground read as one tall stepped wall, and the sky started up to 14 m up. Now the roofs are mostly flat blocks of different heights, and compound yards open to the sky at 10 m. In compound_2 and compound_3 you see low building fronts with a wide sky above them: a yard between buildings, not a pit.
  • Room around the yards. Other rooms now keep 4 extra cells away from a yard at layout time, so the roofs have rock to carve even when a level is crowded.
  • Life inside. Every building now has window openings in its side walls (too low to climb through, like firing slits, so you see and shoot through them), a light, a counter against the back wall in the bigger ones, and a health or ammo pickup inside: a reason to go in. Seeds 1–8: 16 of 16 yards have buildings, 112 window openings in total.
  • All levels validate, 94 tests pass (including: windows too low for a player, every building reward reachable), levels without a style are unchanged, and the crack check is clean (0 pink pixels in 63 shots).

What doesn't work yet

  • The free-standing buildings are taller than the roofs around them (up to 7 m against 5.5–8 m), so from some corners they look like pillars rather than houses. Lower or wider buildings would read better.
  • Interiors are minimal: one room, one counter, one pickup. No second floor, no stairs inside.
  • The skyline is only one band deep (4 cells). Beyond it the rock rises to the sky plane, so the far edge is a low wall rather than a distant horizon.
