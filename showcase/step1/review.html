Step 1: hall cross-sections, before and after

Same 12 levels, same seeds, same camera rules. The layouts are identical; only the architecture pass is new (D18). Each screenshot has its predecessor on the left. No examples were picked or touched up. Where a level rolled a weak profile, it's shown as it is.

What works

  • Gothic vaults read as Gothic. fortress_1 and fortress_2 got a ribbed pointed vault over the whole mega-room: a stepped arch from wall to wall, repeated bay after bay. This is the first time a BadLevel space is recognisable from its ceiling alone.
  • The basilica is a nave with an arcade. fortress_3 has a tall pointed nave, lower aisles and a row of 2×2 piers on each side (10 of 10 placed).
  • Industrial halls get structure you can read. Portal frames (pilasters plus deep trusses) and clearspan trusses turn the flat dark lid into a roof structure. The works decks and catwalks now sit under it, and it starts to look like a place where something is made.
  • The bunker got heavy. Mushroom piers with drop panels, deep downstand ribs and haunched edges. Underground_1 and underground_3 read as poured concrete, not office rooms.
  • Compound_3's multispan (gabled spans on two rows of columns) is the most "industrial building" silhouette in the set.
  • Nothing broke. All 12 levels validate. The layouts, room counts and landmark counts are unchanged, and exposure moved by at most 5 points (the piers are new cover, but most of them stand in halls that were already exposed). Levels without a style are byte-identical to before.

What doesn't work yet

  • Voxel arches. Vaults step 16 units per cell. From across the hall that reads as an arch; from underneath it reads as an upside-down staircase. Smooth vaults need sloped brushes in the exporter. That's a contained change, and worth discussing.
  • Small rooms barely change. On multi-storey levels most rooms are 6 m high, which leaves 64 units to shape after headroom. They get haunches or a shallow gable, and 4–10 rooms per level stay plain. The hierarchy is deliberate, but the minor tier is very quiet.
  • The sample was unlucky for industrial. industrial_1 and industrial_2 both rolled clearspan for their grand hall: deep trusses but no columns. That's a legitimate open volume (Noor's point 4), but less "factory" than portal frames or multispan. Sawtooth was available and didn't come up in these seeds. Across seeds 1–6 the grand halls were portal ×2, multispan ×2 and clearspan ×2.
  • Reveals are unchanged. The corridor-to-hall shots still look down a plain corridor at a wall of the hall. Framed reveals are step 3.
  • Untouched by design: the compound's yards still feel like roofless rooms, the bunker is still not below ground, and landmarks are still only "the biggest room". Those are later steps.
  • Preview lighting renders every ceiling black from below, so profiles show mostly as silhouettes against the walls. A lit compile (still waiting on your OK to run the downloaded tools) would show them better.
