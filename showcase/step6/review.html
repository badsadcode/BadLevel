Step 6: compounds, buildings in the yards

Same 12 seeds and categories. Left: step 5. Right: step 6 (D24). The compound levels changed. The fortress and industrial yards can also get buildings now, as one feature option among the others. A new rooftop camera rule stands on a building's roof.

What works

  • Buildings you can walk into. Compound yards now get 1–3 free-standing buildings: walls, a roof and a doorway facing the middle of the yard, with a room inside. Seeds 1–8: 14 of 16 yards have them. A test checks that every interior is reachable.
  • Rooftops as vantage points. About two-thirds of the buildings have a ladder (25 over seeds 1–8). Up there you look across the yard at the other buildings (the new rooftop shots), and the generator puts a sniper and a reward on the roof. That's compound gameplay: fight on the ground, or climb for the high ground.
  • Safe by construction. The roofs carved around the yard are scenery. They're only cut where nothing else is within one cell, and they're out of step reach of anything a player stands on. A test checks that no roof is reachable from the start. All levels validate, 92 tests pass, levels without a style are unchanged, and the crack check is clean (0 pink pixels in 63 shots).

What doesn't work yet

  • The roofscape barely shows from the ground. Compound yards are packed tightly against halls and rooms, and wherever another space is near, the rock correctly stays full height. So most yard walls still look tall, with a stepped skyline only in places. It shows much better from the rooftops. To really fix it, the yard needs space around it at layout time (a "set back" from neighbouring rooms), not just at decoration.
  • Buildings are plain boxes: one doorway, no windows, nothing inside, no second storey.
  • Ladders show as brown posts in the preview (the entity marker). In an engine they're a ladder volume against the wall.

Questions

  1. Give yards breathing room at layout time (a set-back), so the skyline can actually be seen?
  2. Windows and a little interior in the buildings, or leave them as simple blocks for artists?
