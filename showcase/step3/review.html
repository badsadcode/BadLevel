Step 3: portals and framed reveals

Same 12 levels, same seeds, same camera rules. Left: step 2. Right: step 3 (D21). Corridor openings now get a frame, and big halls point their end feature at the way in. Nothing was picked or touched up.

What works

  • Portals. Corridor openings into styled rooms get jambs: solid members on both sides, so a doorway reads as a doorway in a wall. Bunkers get 2-deep blast frames. In grand and major rooms the mouth rises above the corridor: a pointed arch (gothic), a round arch (compound), a tall opening under a lintel (industrial).
  • The splay. A first version only raised the mouth itself, and the renders showed that's invisible from inside the corridor: the low corridor ceiling hides the frame until you're standing in it. Now the last 2 corridor cells ramp up into the portal, so the opening grows as you walk towards it. In fortress_2 and the industrial levels you can see the corridor lid lift ahead of you. 74 of 75 heads got the full 2-cell ramp.
  • Framed reveals, with surprises. About two-thirds of the time, a big hall's end feature (apse, loading door, blast door, bay window) goes on the wall facing the way in, on its axis. The rest stay oblique. Over seeds 1–5 and the big halls with a recognisable way in: 31 face it, 15 more look straight through to the next portal across the hall (the classic Quake vista, counted separately), and 41 are oblique.
  • Doors still work. A mouth holding a door is never raised, and a test checks that every door still reaches the ceiling of the corridor it closes. All levels validate, 85 tests pass, levels without a style are byte-identical, and the magenta crack check found nothing in 48 shots.

What doesn't work yet

  • The reveal camera undersells it. The fixed camera rule stands 4 cells back and aims at the hall's centre, which is often off the corridor axis, so half of most reveal shots is corridor wall. The 3D previews show the portals better: walk out of a corridor into a hall, then turn round. I didn't change the camera rules in this step, to keep the comparison fair.
  • Fortress has the fewest axial features (6 of 20 face the way in or look through). Its far walls are crowded with wide doorways.
  • Plain frames. Jambs are boxes and arches have no voussoirs or moulding. The axis is the mouth's centre line, not the line of sight from deeper in the corridor. Diagonal corridor mouths get no head.
