Smooth vaults, small rooms, and structure along the walls

Same 12 levels, same seeds, same camera rules. Left: step 1 (stepped cross-sections). Right: this step, which adds three things in the order you agreed: smooth vaults and roofs (D19), more character for small rooms (D19), and structure along the walls (D20). The layouts haven't changed. A few camera positions moved by a cell or two, because the "farthest free corner" rule now steps around the new wall members. Nothing was picked or touched up.

What works

  • Vaults are vaults now. The fortress halls have smooth pointed vaults with ribs instead of stepped "voxel arches". In the .map they're real sloped brushes (convex, ready for qbsp); in the 3D preview and the .glb they're smooth surfaces. Gameplay and analysis still use the lowest point of each cell, so validation is unchanged. A render with a magenta background found no cracks in any of the 48 shots.
  • Small rooms have a ceiling worth looking at. Low pointed vaults, gables and sheds now read as surfaces, and two new low-room profiles (tray, coffered) cover what vaults can't. Plain rooms over seeds 1–5 (secrets excluded): fortress 28 → 6 of 64, industrial ~45 → 13 of 78, compound ~30 → 10 of 63.
  • Walls have rhythm. Members on the bay lines of every wall (gothic shafts, industrial frame pilasters, bunker counterforts) and recesses cut into the solid wall between them (lancet niches with pointed heads, service bays, shelters, window bays). Big halls get one feature on an end wall (apse niche, loading door, blast door, bay window). Tall industrial halls get crane corbels.
  • Measured: the mean longest flat wall per room roughly halved: fortress 11.5 → 5.9 m, industrial 13.0 → 7.0 m, compound 14.5 → 5.9 m, bunker 7.1 → 5.4 m. Each card shows its own numbers.
  • Nothing broke. All levels validate, 81 tests pass, and levels without a style are byte-identical to before (golden check 26/26).

What doesn't work yet

  • Busy small gothic rooms. Shafts every 3 cells in a 6 m room can look crowded. The minor tier only thins the recesses, not the members. Worth your eye.
  • One long wall survives: industrial_1's mega-hall still has a 26 m flat run, where doors and platforms left no room for members.
  • Members are plain full-height boxes. There are no stepped buttresses, string courses or capitals yet, so a gothic shaft and an industrial pilaster are the same shape at different spacings.
  • The bunker is still the weakest. Its 128-high rooms can't take any ceiling work. Counterforts and low shelters help, but it still isn't underground. That's a separate step.
  • Reveals are unchanged (step 3). The preview still draws ceilings dark from below; a lit compile still needs your OK to run the downloaded tools.
