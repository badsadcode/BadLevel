Step 9: room options, bigger rooms, more shapes, domes, spiral stairs

Same 12 categories and seeds as before. Left: today's defaults. Right: the same preset and seed with --space 0.8 --variety 1 (D32, D34). The two sides are different layouts (bigger rooms change the whole plan), so compare the feel, not the room positions. Nothing was picked or touched up. Both sides include the playtest fixes (D33: no squeezes for a real player, fewer monsters in big rooms, brighter Quake light).

What works

  • Roomier, and the default is untouched. Tom: "sometimes the rooms feel a little bit cramped… I don't want to lose what we have." Both dials are 0 by default, which changes nothing (38 reference levels identical, byte for byte). Over these 12 levels: average room side 14.1 → 20.9 m, average height 7.2 → 8.6 m, corridors 3.2 → 3.8 cells wide. The underground halls gain the most: their low ceilings were the most cramping.
  • More shapes. Round, hex, Z, H and diamond plans join the preset's own: 0 of these in the 12 default levels, 37 rooms with options on (round 10, hex 8, Z 11, H 8). Round rooms read as round in the overview: their outline uses 45-degree half cells, not a staircase.
  • Domes. 15 domes in the 12 levels, three times as likely over centred plans (round, hex, octagon, diamond). Built in 3 to 7 courses, a corbelled dome: the new shape shot shows the rings from a room's edge.
  • Spiral stairs. 19 towers in these levels: a ring round a solid newel, every cell's floor following its angle, flat landings at the doors. The spiral shot is a cutaway (roofs off) from above: inside the shaft, at player height, you only see the nearest wall.
  • Same fight, more room. Monsters per level 72 → 64: bigger rooms no longer mean more monsters.

What doesn't work yet

  • Steps are whole cells (32 units deep, 16 high): the spiral reads as chunky, not as thin wedge steps. Curved stairs along a round wall need geometry finer than the grid too (roadmap).
  • Domes are stepped, fine for a blockout or a corbelled vault; a smooth dome needs curved patches (Quake 3) or a mesh in Blender.
  • Most rooms are still rectangles (107 of 176): the extra shapes are an addition, by design, but a level that is all round or all hex isn't possible yet.
  • Megarooms get a dome as often as round rooms do; a 40 m dome over a rectangular hall reads better as a vault than a dome.
